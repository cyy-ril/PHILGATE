Our Purpose

Sustaining Crafts, Empowering Communities

PHILGATE was founded to celebrate and preserve the vibrant tapestry of traditional Philippine handicraft. From the intricate weave of Mindanao's T'nalak to the master woodcarvings of Paete, Laguna, every piece sold on PHILGATE tells a story of identity, skill, and patience passed down through generations.

By eliminating middle-tier markups and providing direct online access, we ensure that local artisans receive fair compensation while keeping Philippine heritage arts alive in modern homes.
